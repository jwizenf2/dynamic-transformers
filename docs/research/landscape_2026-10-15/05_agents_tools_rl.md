## Threats to novelty

1. **2608.18171 already establishes the headline "Ouro + recurrence helps compositional tool calling, more on NESTful, and the gate gives a good trade-off."** Our contribution must be framed as *per-request*, *structure-conditioned* and *frozen few-shot*:
   - (a) stratification by longest dependency path while controlling #calls and length. They only contrast benchmark categories, and BFCL's multi-call categories have no dependencies.
   - (b) the per-request "needed passes" variable (minimum pass count that solves the request).
   - (c) going past T=4.
   - (d) a learned controller.

   Their qualitative Figs 5–9 (2-call NESTful chains fixed at depth 3) come close to C1 anecdotally.
2. **C2 tension:** both 2608.18171 (Fig 4) and 2607.20519 report Ouro's gate as competitive in aggregate. C2 must be stated as "the gate's per-request depth does not correlate with dependency depth / needed passes", not "the gate is bad". Otherwise it contradicts published results.
3. **C4:** TaH2 (2609.35748) trains a per-token iteration decider and evaluates on BFCL. Looped Actor and FlexLoop use state-wise stopping via output-consistency rules. A per-request bandit that uses *structural features of the request* (predicted dependency depth) was not found anywhere. Baselines we must beat: Ouro gate thresholds q, a KL/consistency-based stop (Looped Actor τ=1e-3 KL; FlexLoop ε), and fixed best depth.
4. **C1 precedent outside LMs:** Looped Actor (Boxoban loops rise with remaining pushes, in solved episodes only) is the same phenomenon in RL. Cite it as convergent evidence, not as a threat.
5. **C3:** LoopCoder-v2 shows non-monotonic loops (R≥3 regresses on SWE). TaH2 shows Ouro's BFCL drops from M=2 to M=4. The Ouro paper notes instability at 8 steps in pretraining. "Over-looping hurts shallow inputs" is partly known. Our novelty is the *depth-dependent sign* (deep gains, shallow losses).
6. Our NESTful numbers need a comparability note against 2608.18171: they use SFT (Hermes), a 500-example evenly-spaced subset, and win rate. Ouro-2.6B SFT reaches .371 and Base .190; the Base prompt protocol is unspecified.

## Known dead ends

- **Plain GRPO / RLVR on Ouro.** Ouro authors: DAPO/GRPO gave no gain over SFT (fixed-4 rollouts, and off-policy exits both failed). LoopRPT reports collapse to ~4–7% GSM8K under compute-matched GRPO (snippet, UNVERIFIED in PDF). A 0z5a/vime community GRPO experiment showed no gains, but it was tiny (6 updates, 1 seed). Use RLTT, LoopOPD or LoopRPT-style credit across loops if RL on the backbone is needed. For C4, keep RL in a *separate controller* (bandit) over a frozen or SFT backbone. This avoids the rollout-engine mismatch entirely.
- **Rollout engines with adaptive exit.** vLLM/SGLang assume a fixed path, and vLLM runs all 4 loops for Ouro. Projects in progress: ThinkFlowLab/vllm-rlt (adaptive-exit RFC #56). Per-request (not per-token) depth avoids this, which is an argument for C4's granularity.
- **Steering / loop allocation on frozen Ouro (math):** 2607.18553 found no gain from "exact-compute loop allocation" or minimal LoRA direction-binding. Hidden-state probes still predicted success (AUROC .797).
- **Retrofitted recurrence on deep nesting:** McLeish-style retrofits of Llama/OLMo stay far below Ouro on NESTful (single-digit win rate). They are not a viable control model for depth studies.
- **Looping beyond the trained count in pretraining** (Ouro at 8 steps: loss spikes). LoopCoder-v2 regresses at R≥3.
- **Benchmarks:** Qwen3-1.7B SFT on Hermes scored 0.000 on NESTful in 2608.18171, a format failure. Hermes-format SFT does not transfer to NESTful's `$var` syntax for every model. Check format compliance (Parse rate) separately from correctness.

## Exact recipe we would need to reproduce 2608.18171's SFT

Stated in the paper (§4.1, App. A–B):
- Data: NousResearch `hermes-function-calling-v1` (HF), ChatML, with Hermes JSON `<tool_call>` protocol. 90/10 train/val split.
- Max sequence length 4,096. 2 epochs. AdamW, LR 2e-5, cosine decay, 3% warmup. Per-device batch 2, grad accumulation 2 (effective batch 4 on 1 GPU). **LoRA rank 32**. bf16. 1× A100 80GB.
- Ouro loss: Σ_{t=1..4} p_φ(t|x)·L^(t) + β·KL(p_φ(·|x) ‖ Uniform), β = 0.1, i.e. Ouro's Stage-I objective with the exit distribution kept in the loss. Retrofits instead sample r ~ Poisson(LogNormal(μ,σ²)) per batch, use a final-readout CE, and add β=0.1 KL to the frozen pre-adaptation model.
- Eval: BFCL v3 official non-live AST. NESTful official metrics (F1 func/param, partial/full sequence, win rate) on the full set; depth sweeps on a 500-example evenly-spaced subset. API-Bank Call/Exact/Parse. Fixed depth 1–4 for Ouro. Adaptive mode uses the pretrained gate with CDF threshold q ∈ {0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.8}.
