# Huginn-0125: a second model family (pre-registered 2026-11-14, before the fine-tune)

Why: the depth findings replicate across three Ouro seeds, Ouro-1.4B, unseen NESTful requests and
GSM8K, but only within one family. Dory's loop count acts as a perturbation on this task and
Nanbeige runs only at its trained depth, so neither tests the account. Huginn-0125 (Geiping et al.
2025, arXiv 2502.05171; 3.5B; vendored and audited in `third_party/huginn`) differs from Ouro in
the ways that matter: it re-injects the input at every recurrence step, its 4-layer recurrent
block is a small part of the model, and it was trained with a random number of steps (mean 32),
with gains reported up to ~64 steps and no collapse beyond.

## Model and protocol

* Fine-tune (`huginn_sft.py`, `run_stages.py --plan huginn`): LoRA (rank 32, alpha 64) on all
  linear layers of prelude, recurrent block, coda and the injection adapter; chain v3's stage-2
  data (Hermes FC v1 3,000 + synthetic chains v2 + MathQA train dialogues), 6,000 conversations
  sampled, max 2,048 tokens, one epoch, AdamW 1e-4, effective batch 4; Huginn's chat template; loss
  on assistant turns; recurrence depth per micro-step drawn as in pretraining (mean 16, gradient
  through the last 4 steps). Smoke-tested locally (2 steps, resume).
* Evaluation (`huginn_nestful.py`): NESTful [0, 480), greedy multi-turn dialogues at 4, 8, 32 and
  64 recurrence steps, same system prompt, execution, turn and token limits and grading as
  the Ouro runs; full-plan condition at 8 and 32 steps (`make_nestful_plans.py --n 480 --tag
  480`). Decoding is reproducible (random initial state seeded per position).
* Analysis: `replication_metrics.py --model huginn-chain --nestful-tag nestful --plan-tag
  plan_full --start 0 --n 480 --shallow 8 --deep 32 --stable 64`.

## Predictions

The main step is 8 → 32 steps (two doublings in the middle of the trained range, as Ouro's 2 → 4
is the upper half of its 1–4), with 64 as the deeper check.

* Precondition (reported first): accuracy at 32 steps minus 8 steps, paired bootstrap 90%
  interval. If the lower bound is not above 0, depth does not act as a capability knob for the
  fine-tuned Huginn on this task (as for Dory) and P1–P4 are reported as uninformative.
* P1 formulation: among requests right at 32 and wrong at 8 steps, wrong-plan + wrong-operation
  share of the 8-step errors > .5.
* P2 compounding: the 8 → 32 gain on 6–8-call chains minus on 2–3-call chains, 90% lower bound > 0.
* P3 plan ablation: gain without plan minus with the full plan, 90% lower bound > 0 (the overall
  form; the split by chain length is reported as on Ouro).
* P4 stability: share of 8 → 32 fixes also right at 64 steps >= .6.
* P7 (Huginn-specific) no collapse beyond the main depth: accuracy at 64 minus 32 steps, paired
  90% interval lower bound > −.03 (contrast: Ouro falls from .50 at 4 loops to .20 at 8).
* P5/P6 are not tested (the early-signal probe is Ouro-specific).

Limitations stated in advance: one training seed; a shorter fine-tune than chain v3 (6,000 vs
~19,000 conversations, mean training depth 16 instead of pretraining's 32); 480 requests.

Review before queueing (2026-11-14, applied): the analysis script's error-type bookkeeping was
hard-coded to depths 2/4 (fixed); dialogues are capped at Huginn's 4,096-position rotary table (a
dialogue that reaches it ends unfinished and is flagged; Ouro has no such limit); 16 steps dropped
from the evaluation (no prediction uses it); validation restores the global RNG so the training
depth draws do not repeat after each evaluation. Expected lab time 45–60 h.

## Results (job afda0f9d, finished 2026-11-16; analysed 2026-11-18)

Fine-tune: 1,500 steps (one OOM at step 141 from MPS cache fragmentation, fixed and resumed from the
step-125 checkpoint); validation CE at 4/16/32 steps 1.79/0.92/0.91 before, .293/.184/.183 after.
NESTful [0, 480) accuracy at 4/8/32/64 steps .015/.042/.088/.083 (Ouro chain v3 on the same
requests: .17–.50 at 1–4 loops); with the full plan at 8/32 .329/.396. Calls are well formed
(~4 calls per dialogue, tool errors <= .05, no dialogue reached the 4,096-position cap); the
model mostly sets the problem up wrong (wrong-plan errors 74% of requests at 8 steps).

* Precondition: 8 → 32 steps +.046 (90% CI +.029 to +.063; 24 gains, 2 losses). Depth helps and the
  gain is not noise, but the model is weak, so the remaining tests sit near the floor.
* P1 formulation SUPPORTED: 22 of 24 requests fixed by 32 steps were formulation errors at 8 (.92).
* P2 compounding NOT SUPPORTED (reversed): gain +.088/+.054/+.015/+.025 for 2–3/4–5/6–8/9+ calls;
  6–8 minus 2–3 −.073 (CI −.118 to −.027). Long chains are near zero at every depth (floor).
* P3 plan ablation NOT SUPPORTED: gain .046 without the plan, .067 with it (difference −.021, CI
  −.054 to +.013): with the plan Huginn still fails 60–67% of requests, so depth still helps there.
* P4 stability SUPPORTED: 21 of 24 fixes (.875) also right at 64 steps.
* P7 no collapse SUPPORTED: 64 minus 32 steps −.004 (CI −.013 to +.004); 2 gains, 4 losses. Unlike
  Ouro (trained at 4 loops, .50 → .20 at 8), Huginn (trained with random depth up to ~64) holds.

Reading: Huginn reproduces the findings that do not need a capable model (depth fixes formulation;
the need for depth is stable; no collapse inside the trained depth range) and fails the two that
compare depth's benefit across conditions (chain length, given plan), where its accuracy is at the
floor. With 4–9% accuracy it is a weak second family; the paper reports it as partial support with
the floor effect stated, not as a replication.
